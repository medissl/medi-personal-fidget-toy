# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-25

- Renamed to Medi Personal Fidget Toy; minimal Android menu and two-column selection.
- Full-screen gameplay, tap-only controls that fade after two seconds, and immersive system bars.
- Denser, brighter particles, five modes, ten-touch input, gathering and release bursts.
- Recorded effects, higher audio gain, bounded click playback, and selectable music.
- Added Slime, Buttons, Sand, and Newton's cradle; preserves existing Android installation and preferences.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-25

- First personal Android preview: multitouch particles, floating leaves and water ripples, a reversible bubble pad, and a clicking dial.
- Offline music, touch sounds, haptics, and locally saved sensory settings.
- Signed APK available as a GitHub release download. Physical haptics, phone performance, and speaker balance still need device testing.
