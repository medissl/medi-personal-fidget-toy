No-No Stress adapts getAVG/getCircleFingers touch geometry to JavaScript.
The active StarView.sync(LINES) head/tail update is adapted into particle history.
The WebGL renderer extends it with connected fading segments and small particle light halos.
The simulation, steering rules, and trail shaders are our reconstruction;
the public engine does not include complete Atomus effect equations.
