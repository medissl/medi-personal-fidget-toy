Controls & credits

Tap near the top to reveal controls, then tap an icon to use it. They fade after five seconds. Swiping does not reveal them.

Stardust: hold to gather and release to burst. Fingers are moving checkpoints. Pinch, spread, cross, and move them to bend the currents; nearby groups can join or split the swarms. Shapes emerge from your placement and the particles' momentum. Seven fingers create obstacles in a directional stream.

Music: Floating Cities, Carefree, and Dream Culture by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY 4.0 (creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Music levels were normalized for this app. Recorded effects: Kenney, rubberduck, AntumDeluge, Till Behrend, and StarNinjas (CC0). Font: Fredoka (SIL OFL 1.1). Everything is bundled offline.
